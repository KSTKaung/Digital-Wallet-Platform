## 14. Ledger
An internal service API, rather than something exposed directly to customers.
```
POST /internal/v1/ledger/entries

GET  /internal/v1/ledger/accounts/{accountId}

GET  /internal/v1/ledger/transactions/{transactionId}

GET  /internal/v1/ledger/accounts/{accountId}/entries

POST /internal/v1/ledger/transactions/{transactionId}/reverse
```
**Business operations causing ledger operations.**
```
Transfer
   ↓
Transfer Service
   ↓
Ledger Service
   ↓
Debit + Credit
```
